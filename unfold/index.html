macOS Utility

Unfold

The window-aware app switcher for macOS. Press Option+Tab to switch apps, arrow down to pick a specific window.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Unfold gives you fast, keyboard-driven app and window switching without getting in your way.

Quick Switching

Press Option + Tab to instantly cycle through your running apps. Release to switch.

Window Selection

Press ↓ to expand and see every window for the selected app. Pick exactly the one you need.

Instant Activation

Unfold appears the moment you press the hotkey and disappears when you release. No loading, no delay.

Configurable Hotkey

Don't like Option+Tab? Remap the trigger to any modifier+key combination that fits your workflow.

Launch at Login

Enable launch at login so Unfold is always ready when your Mac starts up. Set it once and forget it.

Lightweight

Minimal memory footprint and zero background CPU usage. Unfold stays out of the way until you need it.
